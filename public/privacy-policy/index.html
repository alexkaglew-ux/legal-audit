If you sign up for The Verdict, we collect the email address you provide. Service providers

Website analytics are provided by Google Analytics; hosting is provided through Netlify. These providers process information in connection with those services and according to their applicable terms and policies.

Unsubscribe and deletion

You may unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter email. To request deletion of your newsletter email address or ask a privacy question, email ak@thelewfirm.com. We will review and process requests as applicable, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

External sites

Links to third-party websites, including Sports Legal Audit and social platforms, are governed by their own privacy practices.

Changes
