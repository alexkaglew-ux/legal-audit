ABOUT ALEX

Sports and entertainment attorney for athletes, families and creative businesses.

Alex Kagianaris, Esq. is a parent-first sports and entertainment attorney focused on protecting athletes under 26 — professional or amateur — and their families in NIL, professional deals and related sports business matters. Legal services are provided through The Lew Firm APC.

His practice spans athlete agreements, endorsements and licensing, NIL and college sports, entertainment, intellectual property and business affairs. His approach is informed by legal practice, business affairs, operating businesses and producing creative work, with attention to how one transaction affects the rights, leverage and opportunities that come after it.

Alex also writes Sports Legal Audit, an independent publication providing commentary and analysis on the legal rules, contracts and disputes shaping sports.
