SPORTS + NIL

Counsel for athletes and families navigating careers, rights and opportunity.

Alex Kagianaris, Esq. works with athletes under 26 — professional or amateur — and their families on sports and NIL matters through The Lew Firm APC. The focus is not only the document in front of the client, but how its economics, rights and restrictions affect the career around it.

NIL + college sports

Review and counseling around name, image and likeness agreements, transfer-related questions, revenue-sharing issues and the commercial rights attached to college athletics.

Athlete and family deal review

Contract review and negotiation with attention to compensation, term, exclusivity, approvals, usage rights, post-term obligations and how a proposed deal interacts with existing commitments.

Professional contracts

Legal review of professional athlete agreements and related compensation structures, with an emphasis on what is guaranteed, what is conditional and what rights or obligations continue beyond the headline economics.

Endorsements, licensing + streaming vendors

Commercial agreements involving endorsements, licensing, media, content and streaming or other vendors, including the scope of rights granted and the effect those rights may have on future opportunities.

Sports business matters

Commercial, rights and business-affairs questions involving sports businesses, brands and counterparties where contracts, intellectual property and long-term strategy intersect.
