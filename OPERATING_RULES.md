# AKESQ WEBSITE v2 — OPERATING RULES

1. `main` is production truth. Do not push redesign work directly to `main`.
2. All v2 implementation happens on `akesq-v2` or descendant feature branches and is reviewed via preview before merge.
3. Preserve the existing domain and Netlify deployment unless a migration is explicitly approved.
4. Alex Kagianaris is presented as a Senior Attorney at The Lew Firm APC; the site must not imply a separate Alex Kagianaris law firm.
5. Legal inquiry CTAs route first to `ak@thelewfirm.com` unless Alex changes that decision.
6. Do not publish client names, logos, matters, results, testimonials, or claims of specialization/expertise without explicit source and approval.
7. Sports + NIL is the dominant substantive lane in v2. Entertainment remains a major lane. Business is selective/supporting.
8. Greece/U.S. cross-border work is an emerging lane. Architect and tag for it now; do not market market leadership or a substantial existing practice without evidence.
9. The Legal Audit is supporting proof of expertise, not the site's master identity.
10. The issue explorer is educational navigation, not individualized legal advice. It may identify common issues, explain what generally matters, surface Alex's published resources, and route a user to direct contact. It must not predict outcomes, establish duties, or purport to form an attorney-client relationship.
11. Do not build a free-form AI legal chatbot in Milestone 1.
12. Prefer data/content registries over duplicated hard-coded cards when practical, but do not introduce a backend/CMS merely for architectural purity.
13. No LinkedIn/Substack/YouTube automation until the homepage hierarchy and visual system are accepted. Automation should nominate or ingest content, not auto-publish legal analysis without review.
14. Design target: Lew Firm family resemblance + more editorial/media energy. Avoid sports-broadcast aesthetics, creator-page aesthetics, and generic law-firm template aesthetics.
15. Accessibility and responsive behavior are release requirements, not polish.
16. Existing content should remain reachable during redesign unless intentionally retired.
17. Do not invent credentials, memberships, practice history, client categories, metrics, or legal results. Flag uncertain biography claims for verification.
18. Book and NIL course may appear as clearly labeled forthcoming products; do not imply availability before launch.
19. Use minimum sufficient dependencies. Current React/Vite stack is adequate unless a specific requirement justifies change.
20. Stop when milestone acceptance criteria pass; do not redesign indefinitely.
